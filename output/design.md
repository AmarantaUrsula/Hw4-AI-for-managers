# Campus Customs — Design (Problem 10)

**Direction: "Old Campus in October."** Yale blue stays the anchor, on warm ivory "paper", with small autumn accents. It aims to look like a well-made ivy league shop for 18–35 year-old undergrads and grad students: smart, relaxed, and proud, not like a sports-merch site.

| Area | What changed | Why it keeps shoppers around and buying |
|---|---|---|
| **Fonts** | **Fraunces** (collegiate serif) for headlines, product names, and prices. **Inter** for everything else. | Serif reads as "Yale" and gives the shop an editorial, premium feel. Inter keeps sizes, filters, and chat easy to scan. |
| **Color** | Yale blue `#00356b` + deep navy, ivory `#faf7f0` with a faint paper grain, **rust** `#9a4a24` for small text accents, **ochre** `#c8892b` for decoration only (borders, icons, leaves). Problem 9's blue/amber/gray stock colors are unchanged. | On brand and warm instead of stark white. All text colors pass WCAG AA (rust 5.8:1, muted 5.5:1). Ochre is never used for text (2.8:1). |
| **Hierarchy** | Announcement bar ("Fall '26 on Old Campus · Officially licensed · 57 Broadway"), a stitched "Y" crest logo, eyebrow labels → serif headings, breadcrumbs on product pages (Shop / Quarter-zips / item), category labels on every card. | Shoppers always know where they are and what they're looking at, so there are fewer wrong turns. "Officially licensed" up top builds trust before the first click. |
| **Hero** | Big serif headline with an italic rust accent, a **polaroid collage** of three real products (cropped 3:4 to hide black photo bars), and a few **maple leaves** drifting through. | It shows the products *on the vibe* (fall on campus) in the first second. The polaroids are clickable, so the most eye-catching thing on the page goes straight to a product. |
| **"Shop the look"** | Four photo tiles tied to campus moments: *Library late nights* → crewnecks, *Crisp fall layers* → quarter-zips, *Game day* → hoodies, *Statement outerwear* → jackets. Each opens the Products page already filtered. | It sells the lifestyle, not just the garment, and turns "browsing" into one tap to a filtered list. That's a shorter path to a product page. |
| **Product presentation** | Ivory cards with a soft lift and image zoom on hover, a "View details →" pill, serif name, Yale-blue price, and the size strip from Problem 9. Product pages have a large framed photo and a bigger serif title. | Hover feedback makes the grid feel alive and clickable, and the price and sizes are visible without opening the product. |
| **Chat feel** | "**Ask Campus Customs**" launcher with the crest. The panel is titled "Campus Customs desk" with a green dot and "Checks live sizes & stock", has paper-style bubbles (an ochre edge on replies, Yale blue for yours), animated typing dots, and opens with a gentle slide-up. | It feels like talking to someone at the counter, not a generic bot. "Live sizes & stock" tells shoppers it's worth asking, and the chat is where the size and alternative answers (and the sale) happen. |
| **Motion** | Hero fade-up, falling leaves, card lift and zoom, panel slide-in, typing dots. **All of it turns off with "reduce motion"** (`prefers-reduced-motion`), and the leaves are hidden. | Just enough polish to feel premium and seasonal without slowing anyone down, and respectful of motion-sensitive users. |
| **Footer** | Navy footer with Shop links (by category), store address, a "Questions? Ask the chat" note, and "*Lux et veritas — and a good sweatshirt.*" | A second chance to keep browsing, plus a bit of Yale wit that fits the audience. |
| **Fix** | Pages now open scrolled to the top (before, clicking a "Shop the look" tile opened Products halfway down). | Shoppers land on the toolbar and the first products, not the middle of a list. |
